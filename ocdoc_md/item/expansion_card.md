# Expansion Cards

Expansion cards are components used to add functionalities to a device, for example a graphics card allows a computer to display content on a screen.

## Usage

Expansion cards can be inserted in the expansion cards slots present in all devices, once added they provide the computer a new component with functions you can access using the [Component API](../api/component.md)

## Types
There are 8 types of expansion cards in OpenComputers, Some of them with more than one tier:

- [Graphics Card](graphics_card.md)
- [Redstone Card](redstone_card.md)
- [Network Card](network_card.md)
- [Wireless Network Card](wireless_network_card.md)
- [Linked Card](linked_card.md)
- [Internet Card](internet_card.md)
- [Data Card](data_card.md)
- [Debug Card](debug_card.md) (Creative Item)
