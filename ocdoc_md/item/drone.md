## Drone

Drones are built using a [Drone Case](dronecase.md) in the [Assembler](../block/assembler.md). They are entity-based "robots", a little cheaper but with more limited functionality. They can **not**  interact with external components!

They have no secondary storage and so are programmed entirely by using an [eeprom](eeprom.md). Once assembled, a drone can be reprogrammed by crafting it with a new eeprom (its old eeprom is returned).
