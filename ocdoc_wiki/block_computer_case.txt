Computer Case
=====================================
The Computer Case. Like their real-life counterparts, they house all of the components that make the computer //run//.

In OpenComputers the cases come in four tiers of which only 3 are craftable in survival. Each with better specs than the last. Below are the styles of the case tiers.\\
{{:blocks:teir1case.png?nolink&128|}} {{:blocks:teir2case.png?nolink&128|}} {{:blocks:teir3case.png?nolink&128|}} {{:blocks:teir4case.png?nolink&128|}} \\


Tier 1
----------
The first tier of case, cannot support much but it is a good starting point nonetheless. 
Tier 1 cases provide room for:

- 1 x [[item:eeprom|EEPROM]]
- 1 x Tier 1 [[item:cpu|Processor]]
- 2 x Tier 1 [[item:memory|RAM Modules]]
- 1 x Tier 1 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 2 x Tier 1 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Cards]]

Tier 1 cases are crafted with:
  * 4 x Iron Ingot
  * 2 x Iron Bars
  * 1 x Chest
  * 1 x [[item:materials#microchips|Microchip]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials#printed_circuit_board|Printed Circuit Board]]

{{:recipes:blocks:tier1case.png?nolink&200|}}

Tier 2
----------
The second tier of case, can do more than Tier 1 but not as much as Tier 3. 
Tier 2 cases provide room for:

- 1 x [[item:eeprom|EEPROM]]
- 1 x Tier 2 [[item:cpu|Processor]]
- 2 x Tier 2 [[item:memory|RAM Modules]]
- 1 x Tier 1 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 1 x Tier 2 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 1 x Tier 1 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Card]]
- 1 x Tier 2 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Card]]

Tier 2 cases are crafted with:
  * 4 x Gold Ingot
  * 2 x Iron Bars
  * 1 x Chest
  * 1 x [[item:materials#microchips|Microchip (Tier 2)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials#printed_circuit_board|Printed Circuit Board]]

{{:recipes:blocks:tier2case.png?nolink&200|}}

Tier 3
----------
The third tier of case, can pretty much do anything you want it to do. 
Tier 3 cases provide room for:

- 1 x [[item:eeprom|EEPROM]]
- 1 x Tier 3 [[item:cpu|Processor]]
- 2 x Tier 3 [[item:memory|RAM Modules]]
- 1 x Tier 2 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 1 x Tier 3 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 2 x Tier 2 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Cards]]
- 1 x Tier 3 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Card]]
- 1 x [[item:floppy_disk|Floppy Disk]]

Tier 3 cases are crafted with:
  * 4 x Diamonds
  * 2 x Iron Bars
  * 1 x Chest
  * 1 x [[item:materials#microchips|Microchip (Tier 3)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials#printed_circuit_board|Printed Circuit Board]]
{{:recipes:blocks:tier3case.png?nolink&200|}}

Tier 4 (Creative)
----------
The fourth tier of case, cannot be crafted in survival and only cheated in using the Creative Inventory or NEI.
Tier 4 cases provide room for:

- 1 x [[item:eeprom|EEPROM]]
- 1 x Tier 3 [[item:cpu|Processor]]
- 2 x Tier 3 [[item:memory|RAM Modules]]
- 2 x Tier 3 [[item:hard_disk_drive|HDD]]
- 3 x Tier 3 [[item:expansion_card|Expansion Cards]]
- 1 x [[item:floppy_disk|Floppy Disk]]

Can be spawned with the command "/oc\_sc". It will spawn a creative case with a tier 3 hard drive, 2x tier 3.5 RAM sticks, a creative [[item:apu|APU]], and an [[item:openos_floppy|OpenOS floppy disk]]. It will also spawn with a tier 2 monitor/screen with a keyboard on top. To spawn this on multiplayer, you need at-least level 2 OP. You can spawn this in single player without problems.
