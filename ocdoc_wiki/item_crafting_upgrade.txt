Crafting Upgrade
==============
{{:items:crafting_upgrade.png?nolink&128|}}

**Provides:** Component API [[component:crafting| crafting]]

Enables robots to use the top left area of their inventory for crafting objects. Items have to be aligned as they would be in a crafting table.

{{component:crafting_area.png|Crafting area}}\\
//(free slots are the crafting area)//  
  
The Crafting upgrade is crafted using the following recipe: 
  * 1 x Crafting Table
  * 4 x Iron Ingot
  * 2 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 1)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Printed Circuit Board]]
