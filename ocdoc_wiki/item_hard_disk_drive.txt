Hard Disk Drive
============
{{:items:hdd1.png?nolink&128|}} {{:items:hdd2.png?nolink&128|}} {{:items:hdd3.png?nolink&128|}}

Hard disk drives (HDDs) come in three tiers, with increasing storage capacity (each tier's capacity is configurable). They can store more data than a simple floppy, but are also more expensive to craft. They can also only be installed in [[block:case|computer cases]] and [[item:server|servers]]. They can *only* be installed in robots during the assembly phase. (Prior to 1.3: They can *not* be installed in robots.)

The Hard Disk Drive (Tier 1) is crafted using the following recipe:
  * 3 x [[item:materials|Disk Platter]]
  * 2 x Iron Ingot
  * 2 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 1)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Printed Circuit Board]]
  * 1 x Piston
{{:recipes:items:t1hdd.png?nolink&200|}}

The Hard Disk Drive (Tier 2) is crafted using the following recipe:
  * 3 x [[item:materials|Disk Platter]]
  * 2 x Gold Ingot
  * 2 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 2)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Printed Circuit Board]]
  * 1 x Piston
{{:recipes:items:t2hdd.png?nolink&200|}}

The Hard Disk Drive (Tier 3) is crafted using the following recipe:
  * 3 x [[item:materials|Disk Platter]]
  * 2 x Diamond
  * 2 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 3)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Printed Circuit Board]]
  * 1 x Piston
{{:recipes:items:t3hdd.png?nolink&200|}}
