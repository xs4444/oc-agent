Motion Sensor
===========
{{:blocks:motion_sensor.png?nolink&128|}}

Allows detecting movement of nearby living entities, as long as a direct line of sight is given. Sensitivity can be configured via [[component:motion_sensor|its API]].

*Added in OC 1.3.2.*

The Motion Sensor is crafted using the following recipe:
  * 4 x Gold ingot
  * 3 x Daylight Sensor
  * 1 x [[item:cpu|CPU (Tier 2)]]
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Printed Circuit Board]]
