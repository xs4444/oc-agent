# Transposer

{{:blocks:trasnposer.png?nolink&128|}}

The transposer bridges the gap between redstone controlled hoppers and [[block:robot|Robot]], allowing [[block:computer_case|computer]]-controlled transferal of items and fluids between adjacent blocks.

*Note that this block has no internal inventory.*

Besides moving things around, it can also be used to inspect the contents of the adjacent inventories, like an [[block:adapter|Adapter]] with an [[component:inventory_controller|inventory controller]] could, and the contents of adjacent tanks, like and adapter with a [[component:tank_controller|Tank Controller]] could.

The transposer can also interactive with player inventory when player stands next to or on it.

The component proxy on a computer provides an api as described [[component:transposer|here]]
