Drone
=====

Drones are built using a [[item:dronecase:|Drone Case]] in the [[block:assembler|Assembler]]. They are entity-based "robots", a little cheaper but with more limited functionality. They can **not**  interact with external components!

They have no secondary storage and so are programmed entirely by using an [[item:eeprom:|eeprom]]. Once assembled, a drone can be reprogrammed by crafting it with a new eeprom (its old eeprom is returned).
