Angel Upgrade
============
{{:items:angel_upgrade.png?nolink&128|}}

This upgrade allows robots to place blocks in thin air, without a reference block, similar to a certain block by a certain mod this upgrade may or may not have been inspired by.

*Since version 1.2.6.*

The Angel Upgrade is crafted using the following recipe: 
  * 4 x Iron Ingot
  * 2 x Ender Pearl
  * 1 x Piston
  * 2 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 1)]]
