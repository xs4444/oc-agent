Floppy Disk
==========
{{:items:floppy.png?nolink&128|}}

Small and cheap storage device that can be inserted into [[block:disk_drive|disk drives]], tier three [[block:case|computer cases]] and [[block:robot|robots]].

The Floppy Disk is crafted using the following recipe:
  * 3 x Paper
  * 4 x Iron nugget/oreberry (with Tinker's Construct)
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Disk Platter]]
  * 1 x Lever

{{:recipes:items:floppydisk.png?nolink&200|}}

The floppy disk can be used to create the [[item:openos+floppy|OpenOS Floppy Disk]]
