Internet Card
===========
{{:items:internet_card.png?nolink&128|}}

This card provides access to 'the interwebs'. It allows making HTTP requests and opening TCP sockets, unless disabled in the config. See the [[api:internet|Internet API]].

Note that the Internet Card is a Tier 2 card and requires an available Tier 2 (or higher) slot to use.

The Internet Card is crafted using the following recipe: 
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Interweb]]
  * 1 x Obsidian
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Microchip (Tier 2)]]
  * 1 x Redstone Torch
  * 1 x [[item:materials|Card Base]]
